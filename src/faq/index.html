Follow the Star FAQs

  • When do tickets go on sale?

    Tickets go on sale to the public on Sunday, November 8, 2026 at 9 am. All tickets are sold online at followthestar.church/tickets.

  • How long does the performance take?

    It takes about an hour to walk through all the scenes. Dress warmly—you’ll sometimes be under cover and other times out under the open sky.

  • Do children need tickets?

    Yes, all attendees need tickets.

  • When did Follow the Star start?

    Follow the Star began in 2002. Since we skipped 2020, this will be our 22nd year.

  • How many volunteers are involved?

    About 120 costumed actors and 80 behind-the-scenes staff members work together to make Follow the Star happen.

  • How many people attend each year?

    Approximately 6,000 people go through the program annually.

  • Who sponsors Follow the Star?

    Follow the Star is sponsored by the Seventh-day Adventist Church. While many people from different denominations contribute to this wonderful event, it is primarily supported by the Adventist Church.

  • How can I join the Follow the Star team as an actor or support staff member?

    We would love for you to join our team, simply click the button below and fill out the form.
