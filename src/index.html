SEVEN NIGHTS OF PERFORMANCES

ONE AMAZING STORY

Performances start December, 2026

Follow the Star invites you to experience the
story of Jesus' birth at our live nativity event!

Come join the thousands of people who have made Follow the Star part of their Christmas traditions since 2002!

Starting and ending in a modern-day home, tour groups are transported back in time to follow the wise men as they search for the new baby King. Visit the temple in Jerusalem, King Herod's palace, a shepherds' camp, Bethlehem, and finally the stable where the Messiah is born. You will never forget your journey to discover what Christmas is really all about!

Tickets go on sale November, 2026 at 9 am and are $2.50/each. All tickets will be sold online. Remember to mark your calendar! Parking is free. This is an outdoor event which runs in all weather. Please dress appropriately.
