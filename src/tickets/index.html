Good to know

  • Please arrive 15 minutes before your window. Check-in opens 15 minutes early and closes when your window ends. Groups leave every few minutes, and you'll join the next group with room.
  • The walk takes about an hour, outdoors in all weather. Please dress warmly.
  • Bring your e-ticket on your phone or printed.
  • Buying for a large group? One order can include up to 10 tickets; for more, place a second order.
  • All sales are final. Tickets can't be refunded or exchanged.
  • Parking is free. Questions? Email followthestarinfo@gmail.com.
